VidStash downloads videos, playlists and music to your Android phone so you can watch and listen offline. Free and open source, with no ads, no accounts and no tracking.

Download APK Source code

Android 7.0 or newer · about 11 MB · updates itself from Settings

Take a look

Dark and light themes follow your phone.

Everything for offline

From a single song to a whole channel.

Up to 4K

Pick the quality you want, or just the audio as M4A or MP3 with title and cover art.

Playlists and channels

Save them in one go, then sync later to get only the new uploads. Auto-sync can run every night.

Sections and chapters

Download only the part you need, or split a long video into one file per chapter.

A real player

Background audio, picture-in-picture, speed, sleep timer, chapters and continue where you left off.

Your data, your rules

Wi-Fi only, only while charging, night-only, and a daily mobile-data limit.

Tidy library

Search, sort, folders, multi-select, backup and restore, and any save folder including an SD card.

Install in a minute

VidStash isn't on the Play Store, so you install the APK directly.

  1. Tap Download APK above on your phone.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser.
  3. Tap Install. From now on, VidStash tells you about new versions and updates itself from Settings → App update.

Privacy

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Nothing about you or what you watch is sent to the developer.

YouTube

To find and download the videos you ask for, the way a web browser would.

SponsorBlock

Only if you turn on “Remove sponsor segments”.

GitHub

To check for and download app updates.

Those are the only places the app connects to. Your downloads, history, searches and settings stay on your phone.

Please use it responsibly. Download only videos you have the right to keep, such as your own uploads, Creative Commons videos, or content the creator allows you to save, and respect copyright where you live. VidStash is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to YouTube or Google.
