<p>VidStash saves videos and audio to your phone so you can watch and listen anywhere, even without internet. It is free and open source, with no accounts, no ads and no tracking.</p>

<p><b>Get videos</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Paste a link, search, or tap <i>Share → VidStash</i> in the YouTube app for a quick pop-up</li>
<li>Videos up to 4K, audio as M4A or MP3 (with title and cover art)</li>
<li>Whole playlists and channels; <i>sync</i> later to get only the new uploads</li>
<li>Download just a section (for example 1:20–4:05), or split a video into one file per chapter</li>
<li>Optional SponsorBlock support to cut out sponsor segments</li>
<li>Subtitles saved as .srt</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Watch and listen</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Built-in player with background audio, picture-in-picture, speed control, sleep timer, chapters and double-tap to skip</li>
<li>Continues where you left off</li>
<li>Watch online without downloading</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Organise</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Library with search, sorting, folders and multi-select</li>
<li>Save to any folder, including an SD card</li>
<li>Backup and restore; import videos already on your phone</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Your rules</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Wi-Fi only, only while charging, night-only downloads and a daily mobile-data limit</li>
<li>Home-screen widget, notification controls, light and dark theme</li>
<li>English and Urdu</li>
</ul>

<p>VidStash reads YouTube with the open-source NewPipe Extractor directly on your phone; there is no server in between. It is not affiliated with YouTube or Google. Please download only content you have the right to keep, and respect copyright where you live.</p>
